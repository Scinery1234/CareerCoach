How it works

  1. Upload your resume once. Sky builds an Australian-format version from your own facts, as a Word file you check line by line.
  2. Paste a job ad. About a minute later: the tailored resume, what changed, how you match, and the gaps named honestly. Then the cover letter, written from your proof and reviewed like a recruiter would.
  3. Talk it through. Sky remembers your goals, your strengths and what’s in the way, so every conversation starts where the last one ended. Nine focus areas, from interview practice to getting unstuck.

Fair questions

Will it make things up on my resume?

No. Employers, titles, dates and figures are locked to your original. If an ad asks for something you don’t have, it says so instead of adding it. You see every change before you download.

Is Sky a real person?

No. Sky is an AI, and says so. When you want a person, you can book a session with a human career coach from inside the app.

What happens after the free week?

It’s $25 a month until you cancel. We email you three days before the first charge. Cancel from Me in the app, two taps, and you won’t be charged.

What does it remember, and who can see it?

Short notes on your goals, strengths, limits and decisions, plus your resume. Encrypted with a key just for you. Nobody reads your chats unless you choose to share one with your human coach. Delete it all any time.

Is this therapy?

No. It’s coaching, and it doesn’t replace a counsellor, doctor or other health professional. If you’re in a hard place, call your local emergency number or find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com.

Who is behind it?

Purposefields, a vocational wellbeing organisation. We think career help should be affordable for everybody, which is why this costs $25 and not $250.
