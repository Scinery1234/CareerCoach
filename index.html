Psychological coaching

Because the hard part of a job search usually isn’t the resume.

Knowing what to do and not doing it isn’t laziness. It’s usually a few things stacked up. Sky has focus areas built on well-tested coaching approaches for exactly those days.

  • Worn out or flat: Recovery goes slowly, and rest comes first
  • Putting it off: Action finds the smallest step you’ll actually take
  • Head full, or thinking the worst: Executive function, Reducing the charge and a structured ten-step CBT conversation

Fair questions

Before you start

Will it make things up on my resume?

No. Employers, titles, dates and figures are locked to your original. If an ad asks for something you don’t have, it says so instead of adding it. You see every change before you download.

Does the extension apply to jobs for me?

It does the typing, not the deciding. On Seek and Indeed it puts your documents and answers into the form and shows how well the job fits. You read it and press Submit yourself. It never submits for you and never opens job pages on its own.

Is Sky a real person?

No. Sky is an AI, and says so. When you want a person, you can book a session with a human career coach from inside the app.

Is the psychological coaching therapy?

No. It’s coaching, built on approaches like CBT and solution-focused coaching, and it doesn’t replace a counsellor, doctor or other health professional. If you’re in a hard place, call your local emergency number or find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com.

What does it remember, and who can see it?

Short notes on your goals, strengths, limits and decisions, plus your resume. Encrypted with a key just for you. Nobody reads your chats unless you choose to share one with your human coach. Delete it all any time.
