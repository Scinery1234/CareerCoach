Questions

Common questions

Is Sky a real person?

No. Sky is an AI, built for AI Career Coach. It says so, and it never pretends otherwise. If you want a person, members can book a session with a human coach from inside the app.

Is this therapy?

No. It’s coaching. It doesn’t replace a counsellor, doctor or other health professional. If you’re going through a hard time, Lifeline is on 13 11 14, any time.

Does it make things up on my resume?

No. The resume writer and the tailoring step use only what you give them. Employers, titles, dates and figures are locked to your original. If a job ad asks for something you don’t have, it tells you, rather than adding it.

What does Sky remember, and who can see it?

Sky keeps short notes on your goals, strengths, limits and decisions, plus your resume and your answers about yourself. It’s all encrypted with a key just for you. You can see, edit and delete it in the app. Nobody else can read your chats unless you choose to share them with your human coach.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep access to the end of the month you’ve paid for. Sky’s memory of you is kept for 30 days after that, in case you come back, then moved to the Trash and deleted after 12 months. You can download or delete it yourself at any time.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The demo gives you a day in the app as a sample member, no sign-up.

Who is behind it?

Purposefields, a vocational wellbeing organisation for purpose-driven people. We believe career development and wellbeing should be for everybody, regardless of money. That’s why the membership costs $25 a month, and a human coach is there by the hour when you need one, not as a package you have to buy up front.
