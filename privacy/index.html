Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

Who we are

AI Career Coach is run by Purposefields, an Australian career coaching practice. When you join, we are the ones holding your information. To ask about any of it, reply to any email from us.

What we collect

Your name and email, so you can sign in (we send a link, there is no password). What you type into the app: chats with Sky, your Compass, planner tasks, notes, your resume and job ads you paste, and the answers you give about yourself. Payment details go to Stripe, who process the payment; we never see your card number.

How it is used

To run the service for you and nothing else. Sky and the writing tools send what you have written to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, to produce a reply. Anthropic does not use it to train its models. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we do not advertise to you.

Encryption and who can see what

Everything Sky learns about you, your resume and your notes are encrypted with a key that exists only for you. A human coach can see nothing unless you choose otherwise under Your context, where you can also see, edit, download or delete everything we hold.

How long we keep it

While you are a member, for as long as you keep it. When your membership ends, your memory is kept for 30 days in case you come back, with reminders at day 7 and 14. After 30 days it goes to the Trash, and 12 months after that it is deleted for good. You can delete everything yourself at any time under Your context.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses a sign-in token stored in your browser. The landing page records which message brought you here, with no personal details.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. The app does most of this itself under Your context. For anything else, reply to any email from us. If you are not happy with how we handle it, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Not therapy

AI Career Coach is coaching, not therapy or medical care. If things get heavy, call your local emergency number, or find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com. In Australia, Lifeline is 13 11 14, any time.
