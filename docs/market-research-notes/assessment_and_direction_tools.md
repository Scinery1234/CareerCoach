# Established career-direction, self-discovery and career-assessment tools (incumbents, as of Nov 2026)

Research method note: WebSearch only. In this cloud session WebFetch could not reach most product/primary hosts (80000hours.org, support.gallup.com, careerexplorer.com, mynextmove.org, nationalcareers.service.gov.uk, pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov all blocked by network policy), so facts below come from search-result snippets of official pages plus third-party pages. Where a figure comes only from an aggregator or a competitor (JobCannon is a rival assessment vendor whose "comparison" pages dominate search results) it is flagged. Prices should be re-verified on vendor sites before being quoted externally.

Reference point for comparisons: our product (The Compass by Purposefields) is a free, account-less, ten-step reflection tool (values -> "I am" identity -> North Star -> needs -> strengths -> idea mixes -> choose -> plan -> first steps -> calendar) with a non-directive Claude chat partner, deliberately no labour-market data, no follow-up loop, AU market.

## Product dossier: 13 incumbents (what, methodology, evidence, reflection vs scoring vs labour-market, AI, UX, pricing, traction, criticisms)

### Takeaway
The incumbent market splits into (a) scored psychometric matchers (CareerExplorer, Truity, YouScience, Plum, Traitify/pymetrics) that output ranked career lists tied to occupation databases, (b) strengths/type reports with no job matching (CliftonStrengths, VIA, 16Personalities) that sell a PDF and increasingly an AI chat layer, (c) free public-sector interest-to-occupation tools with hard labour-market data (O*NET/My Next Move, UK National Careers Service), and (d) reflection-and-prototyping frameworks delivered as books/courses/human advising (Designing Your Life, Working Identity, 80,000 Hours). Almost none offer a guided sequential self-reflection journey like ours; the closest are 80,000 Hours (AI advisor + guide exercises) and Designing Your Life (course format).

### Inferences
- The consumer "career test" category is commoditised: free teaser -> $9–$60 one-off report is the dominant model; subscriptions appear only where there is an ongoing coach/team layer (Gallup Access, Crystal). Trust problems (hidden paywalls, generic reports) are the dominant review complaint across Truity, 16Personalities and CareerHunter.
- AI adoption among incumbents is of two kinds: (1) AI chat layered on top of an existing scored profile (Gallup AI, which reads your 34 themes into every answer), and (2) an AI advisor that also does labour-market matching and follow-up (80,000 Hours). Nobody found uses AI as a non-directive reflection partner over a stepwise self-authored journey; that is our differentiator.
- Hiring-side "career matching" apps (pymetrics, Traitify, Good&Co, Plum) all migrated to B2B employer screening; consumer direction-finding did not sustain them as standalone businesses.

## KQ1: What step sequences do credible frameworks use, and how does our values -> identity -> North Star -> needs -> strengths -> ideation -> choose -> plan -> first steps sequence compare?

### Takeaway
Credible frameworks converge on self-knowledge (values/interests/strengths) -> ideation of options -> reality-testing via information and experiments -> decision -> action plan with support, and the meta-analytic evidence says the elements that most move outcomes are written exercises, individualised interpretation/feedback, world-of-work information, modelling, and support-building (plus counsellor support and values clarification in the 2017 replication). Our sequence covers self-knowledge, ideation, decision and action strongly, but has no explicit reality-testing/prototyping step and no support-building or modelling step.

### Cited Findings
- Brown & Ryan Krane (2000): 62 studies, mean effect size .34; five ingredients individually associated with better outcomes and collectively linearly related to effect size: written exercises, individualised interpretation and feedback, world-of-work information, modelling, and building support for choices in one's social network — [CareerVision summary](https://careervision.org/critical-ingredients-career-interventions/), [FSU tech report](https://career.fsu.edu/sites/g/files/imported/storage/original/application/1bcbeffae96901d7661dc5fd160b7a74.pdf), [Brown et al. 2003 "more analyses"](https://data.zhenzhi.com/details/1/875333320).
- Whiston, Li, Goodrich Mitts & Wright (2017), J. Vocational Behavior 100:175–184: 57 studies, weighted mean effect size 0.352; 19 intervention types; highlighted finding that counsellor support is a critical ingredient; secondary sources summarise the critical elements as counsellor support, values clarification and psychoeducational interventions — [EconBiz record](https://www.econbiz.de/Record/effectiveness-of-career-choice-interventions-a-meta-analytic-replication-and-extension/10011720945), [CDAA best-practice summary](https://cdaa.imiscloud.com/Web/Blog/Posts/What-is-best-practice-in-careers-education--An-outline-of-the-evidence.aspx), [Frontiers in Psychology 2024 review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1461503/full).
- Across meta-analyses: repeated sessions outperform one-offs; expert-led beats self-directed/computer-based; group formats match individual; largest effects on career decision-making self-efficacy — [CDAA summary](https://cdaa.imiscloud.com/Web/Blog/Posts/What-is-best-practice-in-careers-education--An-outline-of-the-evidence.aspx), [CDI practice programmes](https://thecdi.net/getattachment/b64a7fa7-b09b-42f2-b0b8-b481bb466367/240827-Practice-Programmes-docx.pdf).
- Designing Your Life sequence: Workview/Lifeview (values) -> Good Time Journal (energy/engagement = strengths/flow) -> Mind Maps (ideation) -> three Odyssey Plans (choose among lives) -> prototyping conversations and experiences -> choosing well -> building a team — [Stanford Life Design Lab](https://lifedesignlab.stanford.edu/dyl), [HKUST syllabus](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/sites/default/files/IMCE/UG/Course%20Syllabus/Fall_2023-2024/ENGG1400_Fall%202023-24.pdf), [UF Grad Advance deck](https://gradadvance.graduateschool.ufl.edu/media/gradadvancegraduateschoolufledu/pdf-files/Grad-Student-Series-Design-Thinking-to-Reframe-Your-5-Year-Plan.pdf).
- Working Identity sequence: list possible selves -> pick one to test -> small, varied experiments and new networks -> make sense of change; explicitly rejects "plan-and-implement" and introspection-first — [Careershifters](https://www.careershifters.org/book-reviews/herminia-ibarra-working-identity), [Pathwise](https://pathwise.io/?p=4573), [NewSouth Books](https://newsouthbooks.com.au/books/working-identity-unconventional-strategies-for-reinventing-your-career-updated-edition).
- 80,000 Hours: guide exercises -> AI advisor -> optional human 1:1 -> job matching and application tracking (framework is impact-weighted and explicitly labour-market-informed) — [80k speak with us](https://80000hours.org/speak-with-us/).
- Scored matchers (CareerExplorer, Truity, O*NET, NCS, YouScience) use a one-shot sequence: answer items -> scored profile -> ranked occupation list -> drill into occupation profile; no planning or action steps — [UTSA](https://careercenter.utsa.edu/blog/2019/12/19/career-assessments/), [NCS discover](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/discover-your-skills-and-careers), [DOL](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/eta/eta20110203).

### Inferences
- Our ten steps map well to "written exercises" (every step is written), "individualised feedback" (AI reflects the user's own words), and partially to "values clarification" (steps 1–3). They do not implement "world-of-work information", "modelling" (seeing people like you in the role — Roadtrip Nation's core), or "support-building" (telling a friend/partner; DYL's "team").
- The "North Star" and "I am" identity steps have no direct analogue in the incumbents; the closest are DYL's Lifeview/Workview compass and Ibarra's possible selves. The "What I need" constraints step (pay, hours, stress) is itself a soft form of reality-testing, applied to the person rather than to the market.
- Our "Mix ideas" random know+do+love trios is a structured ideation device comparable to DYL's Mind Maps and "ikigai" overlaps; it is unusual among software tools, which otherwise compute matches for the user.

### Gaps
- Could not read the Whiston 2017 paper itself to settle whether its "critical ingredients" are the original five or the three (counsellor support, values clarification, psychoeducation); both are reported by secondary sources.
- No study found that evaluates a chatbot or LLM delivering these ingredients.

## KQ2: Do leading tools incorporate reality-testing (labour-market demand, pay, training pathways)? Is withholding it a recognised stance or a gap?

### Takeaway
Public and scored tools build reality-testing in by default (O*NET Job Zones/Bright Outlook, NCS pay/hours/routes, 80k job matching, Roadtrip Nation majors). Reflection frameworks defer it rather than withhold it: DYL and Ibarra push users to test ideas against reality through conversations and experiments, and the evidence base counts world-of-work information as a critical ingredient. A stance of never discussing demand or pay is therefore not a recognised best practice; the recognised stance is "self-knowledge first, reality-test second, by the person's own enquiry".

### Cited Findings
- O*NET/My Next Move: results filtered by Job Zone (education/training required) and flagged Bright Outlook (above-average growth), green and apprenticeship — [DOL TEN 30-10](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/advisories/TEN/2011/TEN30-10.pdf), [DOL release](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/eta/eta20110203).
- NCS: each matched role links to a profile with pay, hours, duties and routes in — [Oak Park guide](https://oakspark.greenhousecms.co.uk/National-Careers-Service), [NCS explore careers](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk:443/explore-careers).
- 80,000 Hours AI Advisor "recommends matched job openings, tracks applications" — [80k](https://80000hours.org/speak-with-us/).
- Roadtrip Nation shows matched "Careers, Majors and Leaders" (training pathway plus role models) — [Lane CC](https://www.lanecc.edu/media/2269).
- CareerHunter added "enhanced course and degree suggestions" — [Trustpilot CA reply](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/careerhunter.io).
- Brown & Ryan Krane: "world of work information" is one of the five critical ingredients — [CareerVision](https://careervision.org/critical-ingredients-career-interventions/).
- UK/international LMI review: "great labour market information is a critical component of any great careers service" and practitioners must translate it for clients — [UHI international review of careers and LMI](https://pure.uhi.ac.uk/es/publications/careers-and-labour-market-information-an-international-review-of-/); yet theory of how LMI supports guidance is scarce and LMI raises social-justice questions — [Staunton 2022, Derby](https://repository.derby.ac.uk/item/9q418/towards-a-critical-realist-theory-of-labour-market-information), [NICEC Journal 44](https://hubble-live-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/nicec/redactor2_assets/files/72/NICEC_Journal_44_April2020.pdf).
- DYL: "prototyping means gathering information through conversations and experiences rather than physical models" — [HKUST syllabus](https://seng.hkust.edu.hk/sites/default/files/IMCE/UG/Course%20Syllabus/Fall_2023-2024/ENGG1400_Fall%202023-24.pdf); Ibarra: "the more unfamiliar the new possibilities, the more necessary it becomes to learn about them through direct involvement rather than planning" — [Careershifters](https://www.careershifters.org/book-reviews/herminia-ibarra-working-identity).
- 16Personalities Career Suite deliberately avoids job titles, recommending "fields, roles and work environments" instead — [16Personalities](https://www.16personalities.com/academy/logician).

### Inferences
- Our "never judge whether an idea is realistic/well paid/in demand" rule aligns with non-directive coaching ethics but goes further than any incumbent: even the pure-reflection frameworks route users to reality via informational interviews. A middle path consistent with the evidence would be a "Find out" step (who could you talk to; what would you need to know) rather than injecting market data.
- Because the user already enters minimum pay and max hours in "What I need", the product implicitly invites a pay/hours comparison it then refuses to make; incumbents (NCS, O*NET) would show that number. This is a likely user-expectation gap.

### Gaps
- No source found that evaluates outcomes of deliberately withholding LMI; no direct Hooley "reality testing" text retrieved.
- Australian LMI sources (e.g. Jobs and Skills Australia / Labour Market Insights) were not researched here (out of scope for this researcher; flag for coordinator).

## KQ3: How do incumbents handle follow-up, reflection over time, accountability and prototyping conversations/experiments?

### Takeaway
Software incumbents mostly do not: scored tests are one-shot with a PDF; follow-up is achieved by selling into institutions (schools, coaches, employers) that supply the human loop. Only 80,000 Hours (AI advisor with application tracking plus human advising), Gallup (AI coach tied to your profile inside a subscription app), and course-based DYL/Working Identity (weekly sessions, prototyping conversations, "team") provide continuity, and the meta-analyses say repeated sessions and counsellor support are what lift effect sizes.

### Cited Findings
- Repeated sessions outperform one-off interventions; counsellor support is a critical ingredient (Whiston 2017) — [CDAA](https://cdaa.imiscloud.com/Web/Blog/Posts/What-is-best-practice-in-careers-education--An-outline-of-the-evidence.aspx), [EconBiz](https://www.econbiz.de/Record/effectiveness-of-career-choice-interventions-a-meta-analytic-replication-and-extension/10011720945).
- 80,000 Hours: AI advisor tracks applications; users can escalate to human 1:1 advising — [80k](https://80000hours.org/speak-with-us/); advisors recruited 2026 — [80k advisor role](https://80000hours.org/2026/05/open-position-advisor-2/).
- Gallup AI persists with the user's profile inside Gallup Access (subscription) and mobile app — [Gallup support](https://support.gallup.com/hc/en-us/articles/48408264183827-Gallup-AI), [Gallup Access app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1011970469).
- DYL courses run as multi-week classes (VCC 24 hours; Stanford weekly 2-unit) with prototyping conversations and informational interviews built in — [VCC](https://www.vcc.ca/courses/aced-0760/), [Stanford](https://lifedesignlab.stanford.edu/dyl).
- Working Identity prescribes small-scale experiments, new networks, and varying experiments to compare before narrowing — [Careershifters](https://www.careershifters.org/book-reviews/herminia-ibarra-working-identity), [Influence3 summary](https://www.influence3.com.au/blog/working-identity).
- Roadtrip Nation: save favourites to a dashboard; access via institution — [Lane CC](https://www.lanecc.edu/media/2269).
- NCS: save results by email or a code, no account — [Oak Park](https://oakspark.greenhousecms.co.uk/National-Careers-Service).
- Truity/Gallup/VIA sell coach-facing credits and dashboards, i.e., the follow-up loop is outsourced to coaches — [Truity counselors](https://www.stg-new.truity.com/page/counselors-profiler), [VIA consultant](https://hbr.viacharacter.org/account/registerconsultant), [Gallup coaches](https://store.gallup.com/c/en-us/for-coaches/assessments).
- Crystal Knows added coaching plans ($99–$499/mo) in June 2026 — [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/crystalknows/diffs/2026W26).

### Inferences
- Our product's "First steps" + calendar/.ics is a stronger action-commitment mechanism than most scored tests offer, but with no account, no reminder and no return path it cannot deliver the "repeated sessions" the evidence favours. The cheapest incumbent-style fix is NCS-like "save by email/code" or an .ics that also schedules a "come back and review" session.
- Incumbents have not solved accountability in software either; the ones that work route to humans. An AI partner that re-opens the conversation at the scheduled time would be novel relative to everything surveyed.

### Gaps
- No usage/retention data for any incumbent's follow-up features found.

## Synthesis: which methodologies are credible; what incumbents do that an AI app lacks, and vice versa

### Takeaway
Credible: RIASEC interest inventories tied to O*NET-style occupation data, aptitude batteries (YouScience/Ball), Big Five-based profiles, VIA and CliftonStrengths as strengths vocabularies (well-normed, though CliftonStrengths is proprietary), and process frameworks (DYL, Working Identity) whose active ingredients match the meta-analytic "critical ingredients". Weak: MBTI-type (16Personalities) and ikigai quizzes as predictors, though both work as reflection prompts. Incumbents beat an AI reflection app on world-of-work information, modelling, normed scoring, exportable reports and coach/institution channels; an AI reflection app beats them on individualised feedback in the user's own words, values clarification depth, ideation, action planning, low friction and cost, and on an evidence-aligned non-judgemental coaching stance.

### Cited Findings
- Critical ingredients (written exercises, individualised feedback, world-of-work info, modelling, support-building; plus counsellor support, values clarification, psychoeducation) — [CareerVision](https://careervision.org/critical-ingredients-career-interventions/), [CDAA](https://cdaa.imiscloud.com/Web/Blog/Posts/What-is-best-practice-in-careers-education--An-outline-of-the-evidence.aspx), [Frontiers 2024](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1461503/full).
- Expert-led beats self-directed/computer-based in prior meta-analyses — [CDAA](https://cdaa.imiscloud.com/Web/Blog/Posts/What-is-best-practice-in-careers-education--An-outline-of-the-evidence.aspx).
- MBTI/16P validity concerns — [UHS Hawkeye](https://uhshawkeye.com/?p=7566), [Univ. Limerick](https://pure.ul.ie/en/publications/are-we-getting-fooled-again-coming-to-terms-with-limitations-in-t/); ikigai quizzes self-described as non-validated — [JobCannon](https://jobcannon.io/pl/assessments/ikigai).
- YouScience aptitude validity via HumRRO/Ball battery — [YouScience report](https://resources.youscience.com/rs/806-BFU-539/images/Discovery_TheScienceBehindYouScience_Report.pdf), [World Ed](https://skills.worlded.org/?p=698).
- DYL evidence thin but positive on dysfunctional beliefs — [European Proceedings](https://www.europeanproceedings.com/article/10.15405/epsbs.2019.11.19), [Leiden](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/designingyourlife/research).
- VIA scale (35M) and free scientific survey — [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/marking-25-years-of-empowering-global-resilience-via-institute-on-character-nears-35-million-people-discovering-their-strengths-and-launches-workplaces-at-their-best-302304961.html).
- AI incumbents: Gallup AI (profile-aware chat, paid) — [Gallup](https://support.gallup.com/hc/en-us/articles/48408264183827-Gallup-AI); 80k AI Advisor (free, matching + tracking + exercises + human escalation) — [80k](https://80000hours.org/speak-with-us/); 80k rewriting advice for AI-disrupted labour market — [80k podcast](https://pocketcasts.com/podcast/80000-hours-podcast/99d6aaa0-2e41-0135-52f9-452518e2d253/were-updating-our-career-advice-for-the-strangest-time-in-history-benjamin-todd-author-of-80000-hours/37ed187b-7a71-409d-a0c4-45b4820ea535).
- User criticism pattern: hidden paywalls and generic reports — [Trustpilot 16P](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/16personalities.com), [Trustpilot CareerHunter](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/careerhunter.io), [Trustpilot Truity](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/truity.com?page=2).

### Inferences
**What incumbents have that our app lacks**
1. World-of-work information and reality testing (O*NET, NCS, 80k) — a critical ingredient we exclude by design.
2. Modelling (Roadtrip Nation videos; Gallup/VIA strengths communities) — we have none.
3. Normed, scored vocabularies (RIASEC, Big Five, VIA, CliftonStrengths) that give users a shareable "result" and language; our strengths step is free-text only.
4. Persistence and return: accounts, email/code save, dashboards, coach views, subscriptions.
5. Institutional/coach distribution and monetisation (per-test credits, per-seat licences).
6. Exportable report artefacts (PDF).

**What our app has that incumbents lack**
1. A full direction journey (values -> identity -> North Star -> constraints -> strengths -> ideation -> choice -> plan -> first steps -> calendar) in software; incumbents stop at the ranked list or deliver the journey only as a book/course.
2. Individualised feedback and values clarification by an AI partner that reflects the user's own words, 2–5 sentences, one question at a time — closer to "counsellor support" than any PDF, and unlike Gallup AI it is free and not gated.
3. Explicit life-constraints step (pay floor, hours, stress, location) — most scored tests ignore the person's circumstances.
4. Structured ideation (random know/do/love trios) and a commitment device (three <2h actions, scheduled) — rare in the category.
5. Safety design (consent, crisis detection, local AU services), privacy (no accounts/server storage), and plain-language positioning for "everyday people" rather than graduates/office workers.

**Credibility verdict on our methodology**: the sequence is well aligned with values clarification, written exercises and individualised feedback, and borrows legitimately from DYL (ideation, odyssey-style plan horizons) and ikigai/strengths "know/do/love". Its evidence-base weaknesses are the absence of world-of-work information/reality testing, modelling, support-building and repeat sessions. The most evidence-consistent fix that preserves the non-directive stance is to add a "Find out" prototyping step (who to talk to, what to observe, what to try for under two hours) and a scheduled return visit, rather than injecting labour-market scores.

- Primary vendor pages for CareerExplorer, Gallup, O*NET, NCS and 80k could not be fetched; key facts rest on official-page snippets and secondary sources; re-verify pricing before external use.
- Pathways/Yaba not found; Good&Co current status unknown; Traitify ownership conflict (Paradox vs Crosschq) unresolved.
