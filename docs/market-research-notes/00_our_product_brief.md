# Our product: The Compass by Purposefields (repo: /home/user/CareerCoach)

Facts taken from the codebase on 2026-11-06. Treat as ground truth about "our" product.

- **What it is:** a guided, ten-step career-DIRECTION tool (not job search, not résumé) for "everyday people, not just office or tech workers", with an AI thinking partner ("Talk it through") powered by Claude (claude-opus-5-5 for chat/suggestions, claude-haiku-5-5 for quick single-mix ideas).
- **Steps:** Values (pick 5-10 from 45 chips, rank) → Who I am ("I am a/an…" statements, rank) → North Star (free text ≥15 words: #1 value lived by #1 "I am") → What I need (min pay, max hours, stress tolerance, work mode, location, other) → Strengths (know / can do / love, ≥3 each, + other assets) → Mix ideas (10 random know+do+love trios; user writes job ideas; AI "Suggest an idea for this mix"; AI "Suggest ideas" gives 5) → Choose (rank ideas; top pick + second) → Plan (now / 3-9mo / 9-18mo / 18mo+; AI "Suggest a path") → First steps (three <2h actions in next 2 weeks; AI "Suggest 3 steps") → Calendar (day/time/duration, Google Calendar links, .ics download) → Summary "Compass map" (copy as text).
- **Coaching philosophy (system prompt):** non-directive, person does the thinking, reflect their own words, one gentle question at a time, never judge whether an idea is realistic/well paid/in demand, never write their values/North Star for them, "life matters more than job title", plain Australian English, no business jargon, replies 2-5 sentences + at most one question.
- **Design:** plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework/build step. Serif typeface (Newsreader) + IBM Plex Mono eyebrows, teal palette, light/dark mode, desktop = step workspace left + chat panel right; phone = two tabs ("This step" / "Talk it through"). Step strip at top. Pinned action bar with "Still needed: …" instead of greyed-out buttons. Drag-and-drop ranking (SortableJS) with up/down arrow fallbacks.
- **Safety/trust:** consent screen (not therapy; chat is AI by Anthropic; answers saved in browser only; 18+). Crisis keyword detection opens dialog with Australian services (Lifeline, Beyond Blue, 000, SANE, 13YARN). "Help now" button always visible. Prompts live server-side; API key never in browser; per-IP rate limit.
- **Data model:** no accounts, no server storage; everything in localStorage. No email capture, no progress across devices, no follow-up/nudges, no sharing with a coach, no labour-market data, no résumé/job-search features, no payments/pricing (product appears free / pre-commercial), no analytics.
- **Market:** Australian (en-AU, AUD pay, Australian crisis lines). Positioning line: "Work out a career direction that fits your whole life: values, strengths, ideas and three small first steps."
- **Gaps already visible from code (verify/compare against market):** no return visits or accountability loop; no labour-market reality check by design (deliberate stance); no persona/segment targeting beyond "everyday people"; no B2B/coach/employer mode; no localisation outside AU; limited onboarding/landing page (app opens straight into consent + step 1); no export other than copy-text and .ics.
