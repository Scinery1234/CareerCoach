## 2. Pricing and business model

**Membership.** `PRICE.toolsCents = 2500` AUD (`lib/newpage-pricing.js:2`), sold as a Stripe Checkout subscription with `price_data` inline (no Stripe Product/Price object), `recurring: {interval: 'month'}`, `allow_promotion_codes: true` (`lib/newpage-stripe.js:45-56`). Trial: `TRIAL_DAYS()` env, default 7, clamped 0-30 (`lib/newpage-pricing.js:5`); with a trial, `trial_period_days` is set and `payment_method_collection: 'always'` forces the card at checkout (`lib/newpage-stripe.js:51-52`). Quote endpoint `POST /api/newpage/checkout {action:'quote'}` returns `{totalCents: 2500, monthly: true, trialDays}` (`api/newpage/checkout.js:45`); `start` validates name/email and creates the session (`:48-58`); `claim` verifies the session with Stripe, applies it idempotently and signs brand-new members straight in (`:25-42`). Rate limit 20/hour/IP (`:18`).

**What happens at trial end.** Stripe sends `customer.subscription.trial_will_end` three days before; `applyTrialWillEnd` emails `trialEndingEmail` once (idempotent via a `trialend_<sub>` row, `lib/newpage-payments.js:87-98`; copy at `lib/newpage-emails.js:205-215`, says "$25 a month… cancel in two taps: Me → Change card or cancel"). The first real charge arrives as `invoice.paid` with `billing_reason: 'subscription_cycle'`, which extends `access_until` to the period end + 1 day and sends a receipt (`lib/newpage-payments.js:72-84`). Access during the trial is granted as `'1m'` = 30 days from signup (`lib/newpage-payments.js:32`, `lib/newpage-members.js:5`).

**Failed payment.** There is no handler for `invoice.payment_failed` or `past_due` (`api/webhooks/stripe.js:46-57` handles only paid/updated/deleted/trial_will_end; the `payment_intent.payment_failed` branch at `:96-100` is for the legacy booking engine). Consequence: a card that fails at day 7 keeps full access until day 30 (the initial `'1m'` grant) with no dunning email from the app; access only stops when Stripe gives up and sends `customer.subscription.deleted`, which sets `access_until` to `current_period_end` (`lib/newpage-payments.js:101-108`). Since caps are the same during the trial, a trial member can consume up to the full monthly AI allowance (section 4) and never pay. There is also no `charge.refunded`/`charge.dispute.created` handling, so a Stripe refund does not revoke access.

**Cancellation.** In-app "Change card or cancel" opens the Stripe Customer Portal (`app/app.js:456-460` → `billing_portal` → `lib/newpage-stripe.js:59-62`, return to `/app#me`). `customer.subscription.updated` with `cancel_at_period_end` stores `cancel_at` (`lib/newpage-payments.js:111-117`) and the Me tab shows "You've cancelled. Your access runs to …" (`app/app.js:456-458`). Two taps is roughly true (button → portal → Cancel plan → confirm is three or four), but the portal must be enabled in Stripe (README step 3) and the live mode portal config is not in code.

**Refunds.** No refund policy for the membership anywhere live. The old "full refund within 7 days" still appears in the welcome email for program members (`lib/newpage-emails.js:244`), the launch email (`:191`), the day-35 follow-up (`:143`) and the pre-JS join copy (`join/index.html:47`). Under Australian Consumer Law a subscription seller should still state its change-of-mind policy and the ACL guarantees; nothing does. Receipts deliberately carry "no tax wording" (`lib/newpage-program-mail.js:120`), state "$25.00 AUD" and no ABN, so they are not tax invoices; fine under $82.50 only if Purposefields is not required to issue them, but a business customer cannot claim GST.

**Human coach sessions.** README says $250 an hour; `PRICE.hourCents = 25000` (`lib/newpage-pricing.js:2`), max 8 hours per purchase, bought inside the app via `hours_checkout` (one-off Stripe payment, `api/newpage/app.js:587-601`, `lib/newpage-stripe.js:32-40`), which adds to `sessions_included` (`lib/newpage-payments.js:46-56`). Booking uses the members-only `session_types` row "AI Career Coach 1:1 session" (`sql/newpage.sql:242-244`), 24-hour notice, 60 days ahead (`lib/newpage-members.js:6-7`), Zoom personal link, Google/Outlook calendar invites. $250 is above the Bark national average of A$195/hr ex GST but below Sydney's $295 (`australian_landscape.md:144`); the landing page says "$250" without "inc GST". **No cancellation/refund rule for a bought hour** is shown to the member; `tests/newpage-members.test.js:22` suggests a cancelled booking gives the hour back, but there is no member-facing cancel button in the Tools booking flow found in `app/app.js` and no policy text.

**Legacy pricing surface still deployed.** The vikashan booking engine remains: public, unauthenticated `api/packages/create.js`, `api/packages/setup-plan.js`, `api/bookings/*.js`, `api/availability.js`, `api/session-types.js` accept bookings at `TIERS` full_rate $150-250, accessible $65-150, concession $25-65 with a 10% upfront discount and 12-session packages (`lib/booking-price.js:7-9`). Nothing in the public site links to them (only `admin/bookings.html` does), but anyone who finds `POST /api/packages/create` can create a Stripe PaymentIntent for a $65 "accessible" session without membership, and `api/packages/setup-plan.js` attaches cards and charges instalments. This contradicts the "one plan" positioning and is an unreviewed attack surface.

**Positioning against the market.** $25/month AUD (~US$16) sits inside the US$9.99-29.99 consumer band and under Teal+ ($29/mo), LinkedIn Premium Career ($29.99), Careerflow ($23.99) and WonsultingAI Premium ($19.99) (`ai_native_career_coaches.md:165-175`); monthly-only with no weekly billing and no annual plan ("No annual trap", `index.html:162`) is a real contrast with Teal/Careerflow's weekly nudges. The free-week-on-a-card is the common pattern (Career Compass AI also 7-day trial), but every named competitor also has a free-forever tier; this product has only a 24-hour demo with 10 messages. The human-coach add-on at $250 bundles the AI product with a service most AI-native competitors sell as $999-1,999 coaching bundles (Wonsulting) or not at all.

## 4. Usage caps and AI cost

Caps per calendar month (Sydney) for `source: 'tools'`: 300 Sky messages, 3 resumes from scratch, 150 tailored resumes, 100 cover letters (`lib/newpage-usage.js:6`); Python mirror for resumes (`api/_py/np_usage.py:15`). Counted in `newpage_usage` by `(member, month, kind)` with a read-then-upsert (`lib/newpage-usage.js:33-38`), not atomic, so parallel requests can undercount. **Spend is recorded before the AI call** (`api/newpage/app.js:135-140`), so a failed or timed-out generation still consumes an allowance; the demo does this deliberately (`:159`) but for paying members it is a fairness bug. Program/manual members are unlimited (`usageAllowance` returns `unlimited`, `lib/newpage-usage.js:27`). The Python resume limit fails open if Supabase is unreachable (`np_usage.py:48-50`). Nothing throttles LinkedIn/outreach writers, Compass suggestions, `doc_edit`, cover `analyse`/`revise`/`review` (only `cover_draft` and `doc_generate kind=cover` count as a cover, `api/newpage/app.js:135`), so "everything else unlimited" is literally true and those calls are also Opus.

Models and prices (Anthropic list, Nov 2026): Sky = `claude-sonnet-5-5` ($2 in / $10 out per MTok), `max_tokens 4000`, effort low, up to 3 tool rounds (`lib/newpage-coach.js:20,128`); writers and tailoring = `claude-opus-5-5` ($4 / $20) with `max_tokens = maxTokens + 8000` thinking headroom (`:151-156`); resume agent = `claude-opus-5-5`, `max_tokens 32000`, Sonnet fallback (`api/_py/resume_agent/claude.py:27-31,65`). No prompt caching anywhere (`grep cache_control` = 0 hits) although the Sky system prompt (principles + up to 60 memory notes × 200 chars + 3,000 chars of resume + Compass + about-me) and the last 40 messages (`api/newpage/app.js:73`) are re-sent on every turn.

Rough cost per unit (input tokens estimated from the prompt builders):
- Sky message: ~5-8K input (system ~2.5-3.5K + 40-message history) + ~300 output + tool rounds → ≈ A$0.02-0.03.
- Tailored resume: resume JSON + ad (~6-10K in) and 4-9K out on Opus with medium effort → ≈ A$0.15-0.30.
- Cover letter: two Opus calls at effort high (`cover_draft` then `cover_review`, optionally `cover_revise`), ~3.5K out each → ≈ A$0.20-0.40 per finished letter.
- Resume from scratch: Opus structured output up to 32K tokens, multiple passes in `resume_agent` → ≈ A$1-3.

At the caps: 300 × $0.025 + 150 × $0.22 + 100 × $0.30 + 3 × $2 ≈ A$7.5 + 33 + 30 + 6 ≈ **A$75-80 per member-month, three times the $25 price** (plus Stripe's ~A$1.05). A typical engaged member (60 messages, 8 tailored, 4 letters, 1 resume) costs ≈ A$1.5 + 1.8 + 1.2 + 2 ≈ **A$6-7**, so gross margin is healthy at median usage but the caps do not protect the business from heavy users, and the same caps apply during the free week. Switching `NEWPAGE_WRITER_MODEL` to Sonnet (README) halves the writer line; adding `cache_control` on the Sky system prompt and tool definitions would cut Sky input cost ~60-80%.

## 5. Trust, safety and privacy

**Disclosures.** "Sky is an AI" and "Coaching, not therapy. For people 18 and over" appear on the landing price box and FAQ (`index.html:172,179,182`), the join fine print (`join/join.js:40`), the demo (`demo/index.html:45`), the app footer and coach intro (`app/index.html:32`, `app/sky.js:42-48`, `app/app.js:415`), and the welcome/trial emails. Sky's system prompt instructs "You are an AI called Sky. Say so if asked" (`lib/newpage-coach.js:47`). Good coverage; stronger than most competitors in the notes.

**Crisis wording** (commit fb9dfd8). All public pages, the app footer, every email footer and the coach prompt now say "Call your local emergency number, or find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com", with Australian lines as examples (Lifeline 13 11 14, Beyond Blue 1300 22 4636, 13YARN 13 92 76 in the check-in, `app/app.js:553`). The in-chat crisis banner (`app/app.js:421-422`) is triggered by a server `crisis` flag. BRAND.md §9 still mandates Lifeline/000 on every page and Mindframe's standard (two 24/7 services with direct links, `australian_landscape.md:163`) is met in-app but not on the landing page, which lists no number at all. The landing page is the only place a non-member in distress will read; consider at least Lifeline + 000 for AU traffic since ads are AU-targeted.

**Memory encryption.** Envelope AES-256-GCM: per-member data key wrapped by `NEWPAGE_MEMORY_KEY` (Vercel env), AAD binds ciphertext to member and field, key rotation via `_OLD`, crypto-shredding on purge (`lib/newpage-crypto.js:1-91`). Encrypted: Sky chats, Sky's notes, resume profile, about-me answers, private notes, shares (`lib/newpage-vault.js`, `api/newpage/app.js:689`). **Not encrypted**: `newpage_docs` (generated resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn profiles, reports), Compass answers in `newpage_tools`, tasks, goals, check-ins, feedback. The landing page says "Everything Sky learns is encrypted with a key just for you" (`index.html:156`) and the FAQ "Short notes… plus your resume. Encrypted" (`:181`); the memory page honestly says "Your Compass, documents, tasks and progress are separate" (`memory/memory.js:34`). The resume *documents* (name, address, phone, employment history) are the most identifying data in the system and sit in plaintext. `lib/newpage-crypto.js:17-19` itself notes "this is not end-to-end encryption, and the privacy page should say so" — there is no privacy page.

**Data retention and deletion.** Members: in-app "Delete everything" (`app/context.js:119-120` → `data_delete`, `api/newpage/app.js:700-704`) erases Sky memory/profile/about/steer and the data key but leaves the member row, docs, tasks, Compass, bookings and Stripe customer. Ex-members: `/memory?t=` page with export and delete (`api/newpage/memory.js`). Automatic: 30 days active → Trash → purge at 12 months with key deletion (`lib/newpage-retention.js:12`). There is no "close my account" that removes the account, email or Stripe customer, and no stated retention for docs/payments/access logs. Demo members are deleted after 24 h by the retention cron (`lib/newpage-demo.js:99-104`, cascades `sql/newpage.sql:134-222`), feedback kept with the demo id.

**Australian Privacy Act / OAIC.** As a paid service holding personal (and, via check-ins about mood/stress, potentially sensitive) information, Purposefields is an APP entity (small-business exemption does not apply to businesses trading in personal information or likely over $3M turnover; regardless, a consumer-facing AI coach should comply). Gaps: (1) no privacy policy at all, so APP 1 is unmet and the ADM-transparency requirement from 10 Dec 2026 (`australian_landscape.md:175,189`) cannot be met; (2) APP 8 cross-border disclosure: data is processed by Vercel (functions region unspecified), Supabase (project region unspecified in README), Anthropic (US), Resend, Stripe and Google Calendar/Outlook if configured, none disclosed; (3) APP 5 collection notice absent at the join form (`join/index.html:33-48` collects name/email with no notice); (4) no privacy impact assessment or statement that chats are not used for model training (OAIC AI guidance, `australian_landscape.md:173`); (5) check-in questions about feeling low/struggling (`app/app.js:553-563`) collect health-adjacent information with no consent language. Note Anthropic's API has zero-data-retention and no-training defaults for API traffic; the page could say so and does not.

**Demo exposure and abuse limits.** `POST /api/newpage/demo {action:'start'}` creates a real `newpage_members` row with sample content and a 30-day session JWT (`lib/newpage-demo.js:50-71`, `signSession` TTL 30d at `lib/newpage-auth.js:29-31` although access_until is 24 h). Per-visitor 10 messages / 2 documents, global 400 messages / 60 documents per day (`DEMO.caps`, `DEMO.dailyCap`, `:12-13`), optional invite codes with max uses. Rate limits 5 starts/hour and 30 calls/hour per IP (`api/newpage/demo.js:48,57`). Demo visitors can reach everything except payments, booking, email settings and sharing (`api/newpage/app.js:150-160`). Weak points: rate limits are in-memory per serverless instance and reset on cold start (`lib/security.js:1-24`), so the 5-starts/hour limit is cosmetic on Vercel; the daily cap is enforced only by a Supabase aggregate query over `created_at >= now-24h`, so the hard ceiling is roughly 60 Opus documents + 400 Sonnet messages ≈ A$25-30/day of AI spend from anonymous traffic; demo ids are `Math.random()`-based (`:73-75`), not `crypto.randomUUID()`; sample chats/notes are inserted in plaintext and then re-encrypted row by row (`api/newpage/demo.js:68-76`).

**Authentication.** Members: passwordless magic link (15 min) → 30-day session JWT, signed with an HMAC-derived secret so a member token cannot pass the admin check (`lib/newpage-auth.js:1-31`); sign-in request does not reveal membership (`api/newpage/auth.js:11-33`). Session tokens live in `localStorage` (`join/join.js:82`). Admin: single `ADMIN_EMAIL` + bcrypt hash (cost 12), timing-safe dummy hash on wrong email, 5-strike 15-minute lockout (in-memory), 8-hour JWT (`api/auth/login.js`, `lib/auth.js`). Every `api/admin/*`, `api/newpage/members.js`, `content.js`, `codes.js`, `health.js` and the demo GET check `requireAdmin`. No MFA, no admin audit log, one shared admin identity.

**Other security notes.** `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` on all `/api/*` (`vercel.json:36-50`) with bearer tokens means any origin can call the member API if it has a token; harmless for CSRF but unnecessary. CSP permits `'unsafe-inline'` scripts (`vercel.json:82`). Stripe webhook verifies signatures with the raw body (`api/webhooks/stripe.js:24-32`) and is idempotent via `newpage_payments.stripe_session_id` (`lib/newpage-payments.js:22-25,77,91`); failures return 500 so Stripe retries. Crons check `CRON_SECRET` bearer (`reminders.js:11`, `retention.js:15`, `programmail.js:15`). Honeypot field on join and demo. Secrets are env-only; `.gitignore` covers `.env`. Supabase RLS is enabled on every table with no policies, i.e. only the service key can read (`sql/newpage.sql:80-407`, `sql/schema.sql:184-190`), correct for this server-only design. `claim` signs in whoever presents a fresh checkout session id without proving the email (`api/newpage/checkout.js:33-37`); low risk because the id only appears in the payer's success URL.

## Strengths

- Clear, honest price framing: trial, charge date, cancellation path, caps and currency are all on the landing page, join page, welcome email and trial-end email; monthly only, no weekly billing, no annual lock-in.

## Weaknesses

- No privacy policy, terms, refund policy, ABN or company identity; footer links 404.
- Conversion tracking is half-wired (no purchase/StartTrial event); no consent or tracking disclosure.
- Emails and the share image still say "new page" / vikashan.com.au; program-era copy ($500, refund, scholarships, "a session with me") survives in live code paths.
- No failed-payment handling; trial users keep 30 days of access and full caps on a card that may never charge.
- Cap-month AI cost (~A$75-80) exceeds price 3×; no caching; usage charged before success.
- Resume documents and Compass data are not encrypted despite "everything… encrypted" marketing.
- Legacy public booking API with $65-250 tiers and instalment plans still deployed.
- Rate limiting and lockouts are per-instance in-memory, so ineffective on Vercel.
- No lifecycle emails between welcome and departure (no usage nudges, no win-back, no card-failed notice).
- Single shared admin login without MFA.
