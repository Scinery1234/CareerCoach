# Compass by Do With Life: brand notes

The brand for the Compass landing page at `/compass`. It is deliberately unlike the ruled-paper look in `BRAND.md`, which still governs the home page, the member app and emails.

## The idea

**A night sky and one warm needle.** When you don't know what to do next, you don't need the whole map, just a bearing. The page is dark and calm, the only hot colour is the needle, and the quiz makes the needle move.

## Names

| Name | Use |
|---|---|
| **Compass** | The brand and the product. The wordmark is lowercase: **compass**. In sentences, "the Compass". |
| **by Do With Life** | The endorsement, small caps beside the wordmark. The headline plays on it: "Work out what to *do with life.*" |
| **Your bearing** | The two-minute quiz and its result. Never "test", "assessment" or "type". |
| Recharge, Explore, Unstick, Launch | The four places a bearing can land. Shown only after the quiz. Always "you're in Explore", a place right now, never a label. |

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#0B1020` | Background |
| Night 2 | `#141B33` | Cards and panels |
| Line | `#2B3558` | Borders, dividers, the dial |
| Star | `#F5F1E8` | Text |
| Mute | `#A9B0C7` | Secondary text |
| North | `#FF7A45` | The needle and the primary button. Nothing else. |
| Gold | `#FFD68A` | Italic emphasis in headlines, selected answers, links |

After the quiz, the result takes its quarter's colour (lilac, sky, tangerine, lime: the same four as `BRAND.md`).

## Type

- **Instrument Serif** for headlines, large and tight, with one phrase in gold italic per heading.
- **DM Sans** for everything else: 17px body, 12px uppercase letter-spaced labels.

## Shape and motion

- Pill buttons, 16 to 22px corners on cards, thin 1.5px borders.
- The dial is the logo come to life: it wanders before the quiz, turns with each answer, and the dot lands in a quarter at the end.
- Motion is switched off for people who ask for reduced motion.

## Voice

Same as `BRAND.md` section 8: warm, plain, specific, no hype, no exclamation marks, no promised outcomes, no invented numbers or testimonials. Buttons say what happens: "Find my bearing", "Start the Compass, free for 7 days".

## Rules that carry over

- "Coaching, not therapy" and the helpline line are in the footer.
- If someone answers "I'm really struggling", support comes first and every sales prompt is removed from the page.
- The quiz result is free, with no email wall.
