    Fair questions

    Before you start

    Is the two-minute quiz really free?

    Yes. No email, no account, no paywall on the result. Your answers are scored in your browser and stay on this device.

    Is this a personality test?

    No. The quiz shows where you are right now on two things, clarity and energy, and that changes. The Compass itself is a guided exercise built on your own words. Neither puts you in a type.

    Will it tell me which job to do?

    It won’t pick for you. It helps you build ideas from your values and strengths, choose one to test, and take the first steps. It never says whether an idea is realistic or well paid; that part is yours to check.

    How long does the Compass take?

    Ten short steps. Some people do it in an evening, most spread it over a week or two. It saves as you go.

    Is it therapy?

    No. It’s coaching, and it doesn’t replace a counsellor, doctor or other health professional. If you’re in a hard place, call your local emergency number or find a helpline near you at findahelpline.com.
